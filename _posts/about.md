## Our Vision

Àkàndé was born from a fundamental belief: modern voice interfaces should be responsive, private, and deeply intelligent. By combining OpenAI's GPT models with a high-performance Python runtime and SQLite caching architecture, Àkàndé delivers near-instant response times while respecting user data confidentiality.

## Open-Source Innovation

Released under dual Apache-2.0 and MIT open-source licensing, Àkàndé empowers developers worldwide to inspect, extend, and integrate voice capabilities into their own tools, services, and devices.
